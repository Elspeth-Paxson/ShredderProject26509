Welcome

Welcome to our senior design project website!

The U of A EDC and Catalyst Studios produce approximately 50 kg of waste each semester. Much of this waste is recyclable PLA, but the large volume makes it challenging to store and transport efficiently.

Before PLA can be recycled, it must first be shredded into smaller pieces. Our team is building on a previous project to develop a shredder specifically designed for makerspace use, making PLA waste easier to process and helping support more accessible and efficient recycling.

Project Goals

  • Improve shredding performance by reducing the size of 3D-printed waste by 70%
  • Increase system reliability with an improved blade assembly, jam detection, and automatic reversal
  • Enhance user safety with a keyed switch and improved system controls
  • Simplify maintenance through vacuum-assisted cleaning
  • Support plastic recycling efforts by making PLA waste easier to process and manage
  • Deliver a fully operational system by December 2026

About the Project

The 3D Plastic Shredder Version II is a senior engineering capstone project focused on converting 3D plastic prints into a compactable material. This project improves upon a previous team's designs to create a more efficient and reliable shredding system while still being user-friendly and safe!
